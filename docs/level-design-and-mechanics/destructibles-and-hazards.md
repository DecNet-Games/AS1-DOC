# Destructibles & Environmental Hazards

- **`ExplosiveBarrel.cs`**: Detonates when shot, triggering a fiery radial shockwave that damages surrounding zombies and ignites adjacent barrels in chain reactions.
- **`DestructibleCrate.cs`**: Fractures into dynamic wooden fragments when struck by bullets, melee attacks, or explosions.
- **`FlickeringLight.cs`**: Realistic fluctuating fluorescent light flicker with sound hum.
