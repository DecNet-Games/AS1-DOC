# Level Design & Sector 07 Mechanics

Sector 07 Proving Grounds is an expertly engineered modular test facility designed to showcase every gameplay system in AS1 under calibrated conditions.

![Sector 07 Facility Architecture & Stations]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/sector07-facility-overview.png)

---

## Proving Grounds Architectural Zones

The facility features distinct training zones separated by high blast perimeters:
- **Operations Tower (Station 07)**: Central landmark offering 360-degree observation of all proving zones.
- **Armory & Calibration Counters (Station 01)**: Weapon acquisition and ballistics calibration.
- **Precision Target Range (Station 02)**: Scaled reactive targets at 10m, 20m, and 35m.
- **Zombie Drill Court (Station 03)**: High-intensity close-quarters combat zone with NavMesh pathing.
- **Mobility Lab (Station 04)**: Physical obstacles, elevated stairways, and surface footstep strips.
