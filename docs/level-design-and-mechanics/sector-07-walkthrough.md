# Sector 07 Station Guide

Sector 07 is organized into distinct functional zones:

1. **00 | Foundation & Perimeter**: Spawn point and boundary navigation bounds.
2. **01 | Arrival & Weapons Gallery**: Weapon racks featuring 5 configured firearms.
3. **02 | Precision Range**: Calibrated silhouette targets at 10m, 20m, and 35m.
4. **03 | Operations Tower**: Central navigational landmark with rooftop stairs.
5. **04 | Combat Court & Live AI Drill**: Amber terminal console that spawns hostile zombies.
6. **05 | Movement Loop & Surface Audio**: Test strip featuring Concrete, Dirt, Metal, and Wood.
7. **06 | Blast Yard & Recovery**: Destructible physics crates and explosive red barrels.
8. **07 | Extraction Bunker**: Heavy blast door that unlocks once 3 hostiles are neutralized.
