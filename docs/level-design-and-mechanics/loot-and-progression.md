# Loot Tables & Progression

- **`LootDropManager.cs`**: Drops ammo boxes and medikits when enemies die based on configurable percentage tables.
- **`BunkerUnlockSystem.cs`**: Monitors objective counts (`Kills: 3/3`), raises the bunker blast gate, and sounds the victory siren.
