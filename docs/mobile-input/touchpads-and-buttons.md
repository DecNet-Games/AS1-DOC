# Touchpads & Action Buttons

- **`DecnetTouchPad.cs`**: Covers the right half of the display. Tracks finger delta movements to provide smooth camera pitch and yaw.
- **`DecnetButtonHandler.cs`**: Handles touch down, touch up, and hold states for Jump, Sprint, Fire, ADS, and Reload actions with zero touch ghosting.
