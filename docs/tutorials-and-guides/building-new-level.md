# Building a New Combat Level

1. Open `Tools > AS1 > Suite Hub` -> **[LVL] Level Builder**.
2. Enter your level name and select a Day or Night profile.
3. Click **Generate Starter Scene**.
4. Decorate the environment with Sector 07 architectural modules.
5. Bake NavMesh under `Window > AI > Navigation`.
6. Add the scene to `EditorBuildSettings`.
