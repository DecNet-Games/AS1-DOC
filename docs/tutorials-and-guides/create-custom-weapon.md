# Adding a Custom Firearm

1. Open `Tools > AS1 > Suite Hub` -> **[GUN] Weapon Creator Pro**.
2. Select a baseline preset (e.g. **Assault Rifle**).
3. Drag your firearm FBX into the **3D Weapon Mesh** field.
4. Tune damage, fire rate, recoil kick, and assign audio clips.
5. Click **Generate Complete Weapon Asset**.
6. Inspect the generated equipped prefab in `Assets/AS1/Prefabs/Weapons/` and confirm socket transforms (`MuzzleSocket`, `LeftHandSocket`, `CasingSocket`).
